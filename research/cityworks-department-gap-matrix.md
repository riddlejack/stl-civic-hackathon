# Cityworks department and service gap matrix

## Summary

**Observed (local files, retrievals through 2026-10-14 13:53 CDT):** the QE catalog contains 87 saved-search layers—32 Work Order, 42 Inspection, 8 Service Request, and 5 Case Object—but no department-owner field or crosswalk. The separate statement that 21 departments and agencies briefed data to the mayor is **reported** by COO Ben Jonsson; it does not mean this catalog covers 21 departments. [QE catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json), [CityStat launch video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1H60btjS2E)

**Observed:** two substantive completion-source gaps are now narrowed: sidewalk repair and city-tree trimming. Together those service rows represent 751 of the 61,845 hand-offs. QE78 yields same-address later-finish candidates for 16 of 186 sidewalk hand-offs. In a separate R1 tree cohort, 89 of 572 converted requests have chronology-valid finish candidates; all 89 are trimming. The R1 cohort differs from the matrix's 565 tree hand-offs and is not combined with it. Neither source returns a direct request key, and no general citywide closed-removal layer was found. The 25-row matrix covers 46,798 hand-offs (75.7%); 15,047 remain outside it. Three debris rows covering 4,312 are request-only; 20 rows covering 41,735 have no named completion layer. [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip), [QE78](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78), [QE catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

**Observed:** prior public sources provide bounded completion evidence for five service rows. R1's 13.5% traceability figure—8,363 of 61,845—remains frozen under its original sources, cohort, and contract; later tree-planting address/spatial matches cannot be added mechanically. [Forestry ledger](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/forestry-service-records.csv), [board-ups](https://maps8.stlouis-mo.gov/arcgis/rest/services/BUILDING_DIVISION/BldgDiv_Boardups/MapServer/0), [plantings](https://maps8.stlouis-mo.gov/arcgis/rest/services/PDA/City_of_Saint_Louis_Planted_Trees/MapServer/0), [demolitions](https://maps8.stlouis-mo.gov/arcgis/rest/services/BUILDING_DIVISION/Demolitions/MapServer/0)

## What the catalog does and does not establish

**Observed:** the full-extract manifest runs from 2026-10-14 18:48:16 through 18:53:39 UTC and contains no personal fields outside the requested field list. This report treats source availability, a completion row, the link back to a 311 request, and service-wide coverage as separate tests. A same-address or 25-metre match is a candidate link; failure to match does not establish that work was undone. [QE service](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

**Observed:** the catalog is a collection of saved searches, not a department inventory. Forty of its 42 Inspection entries are ten repeated sets of “Priority 1–4, PCA”; bounded probes of the ten Priority-1 copies each returned the same count of four and the same three probed inspections, reinforcing that these are repeated views rather than 40 distinct departments. Names cluster around BPS/PCA cases, forestry/tree work, sidewalks, speed control, and debris; there is no named completion layer for refuse, towing, water, cave-ins, street lights, graffiti, homeless outreach, or sirens. Metadata counts as a lead only: it establishes that a field is advertised, not that a query returns it, that it is populated, or that its date means physical completion. [QE catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json), [QE23](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/23)

**Observed:** all 32 Work Order layers were retrieved with equal before/after counts, producing 36,490 saved-search memberships and 29,018 distinct work orders after deduplication. All 42 full extracts passed before/after count and unique-id checks. The result changes many layers from metadata candidates into available rows, while leaving direct request linkage and outcome semantics to be tested service by service. [QE catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

**Observed:** the locally saved public-map search found QE references only for layers 19 and 20, in the City's public ARPA sidewalk map. This disproves a blanket claim that all QE layers are unpublished. It does not establish publication status for the other 85 layers; describing those as internal-only remains **inferred**, not confirmed. [ARPA sidewalk map](https://geoportal.stlouis-mo.gov/portal/home/item.html?id=50524b4b8be647d8aaad9d38b934bd5f), [map configuration](https://geoportal.stlouis-mo.gov/portal/sharing/rest/content/items/50524b4b8be647d8aaad9d38b934bd5f/data?f=json)

**Observed:** QE19 returned 663 scheduled-queue rows and QE20 returned 217 completed-queue rows. All 880 RequestIds match the local 311 data exactly; filings run from 2014 through 2023, so none belongs to the 2025–26 analysis cohort. QE19 has 649 rows marked CLOSED and QE20 has 215. Their returned `DateTimeClosed` values agree with the local 311 close timestamps for those closed requests, so that field is duplicate request-closure evidence. The layers do not return `ActualFinishDate`; completed-layer membership is a city-reported queue status without an independent dated repair outcome in the retrieved field set. [QE19](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/19), [QE20](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/20), [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Observed:** QE78 returned 1,738 unique CLOSED sidewalk work orders; 1,627 have `ActualFinishDate`, including 633 finishes in 2025–26, spanning 2019-04-10 through 2026-10-01. None of the 49 same-address candidate work-order finishes equals the 311 close timestamp. This rejects literal timestamp duplication but does not prove semantic independence; the returned schema has no `RequestIds`. QE79 returned 1,011 CLOSED inspection rows with `ActFinishDate`; those dates finish inspections, not sidewalk replacement. Three public non-QE Cityworks work-order schemas advertise `RequestIds`, yet five-row probes did not return that field. A direct key is stronger than address/spatial inference, and a named date still needs record-level semantic validation. [QE78](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78), [QE79](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/79), [IRA map configuration](https://geoportal.stlouis-mo.gov/portal/sharing/rest/content/items/f2332491fba944878d838f8141407b97/data?f=json)

**Observed:** 27 forestry Work Order searches returned 34,739 saved-search memberships, deduplicating to 27,267 work orders: 16,778 have non-CLOSED status and 10,489 are CLOSED. Through the 2026-10-13 cutoff, the CLOSED union has 5,961 calendar-valid `ActualFinishDate` values in 2025–26. Separately, 94 non-CLOSED records carry finish dates, including 32 in the current window. Another 565 CLOSED records lack that date and one carries invalid year 4152. Across the union, 1,750 finishes precede work-order initiation, including 565 in the current window; a calendar-valid date is therefore not automatically chronology-valid completion evidence. Saved-search titles do not consistently describe row status, and the schemas have no `RequestIds`. [QE catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json), [QE63](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/63), [QE89](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/89), [QE108](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/108)

**Observed:** the separate R1 cohort contains 572 converted tree work-order requests, while the matrix's N2 basis contains 565 tree hand-offs; their definitions differ. Requiring a compatible operation, work-order initiation from one day before filing through one day after request closure, and the same normalized address or at most 30 projected metres (Web Mercator) found 105 candidate links. Eighty-nine have a chronology-valid `ActualFinishDate` through 2026-10-13, including 74 exact-address links; all 89 are trimming. Six finishes are by the request promise and 83 are later, with a median 218 days late. A contextual comparison using any operation matched 87 of 29,797 unrelated requests. The rolling trim search omits early completions from the comparison period, so these matched delay statistics are not representative service-performance rates. These results narrow the tree-trimming gap; they do not supply a direct key or a general citywide tree-removal completion source. [QE forestry searches](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json), [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Observed:** among 294 removal requests filed in 2025, the complete current-open removal queue has eight exact-address and ten address-or-30-projected-metre after-filing candidates. This improves pending-work coverage over earlier samples, but open-queue absence does not prove completion. [QE63](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/63), [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Observed:** QE18 returned 19,291 unique active service requests across the 61 exact safe operational codes queried; 18,993 (98.5%) join directly to public 311 RequestIds. Those matches span 22 non-null public-311 division labels, which are routing labels rather than verified departments. Returned workflow timestamps add queue visibility, but the schema has no actual-start or actual-finish field, only two rows have `DateTimeClosed`, and 9,165 records still open in public 311 carry `Completed=1`. Another 315 locally open 2025–26 requests are absent from QE18, and absence is not proof of completion. [QE18](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/18), [311 catalog](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/datasets/dataset.cfm?id=5)

**Observed:** QE18 is therefore a cross-cutting routing source for nearly every safe service row, not a service-specific completion layer. In the CSV, “none named” means no named completion layer; it does not deny that a service can appear in this generic active-request queue. [QE18](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/18)

**Observed:** the speed-control work-order searches do not close the signal/sign gap. QE13, QE15, and QE16 returned zero rows; QE14 returned 13 `IProcess` orders initiated in September 2026, all without actual start, actual finish, or work-order close dates. QE106 returned 16 open debris requests, also request/pending evidence rather than independent completion. [QE13](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/13), [QE14](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/14), [QE106](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/106)

**Observed scope limit:** 15,047 hand-offs, or 24.3% of the 61,845 denominator, fall outside the 25-row register's 64 problem codes and were not independently searched in QE. The five largest residual codes are Leash Law (932), Stray Dog At Large (865), Other Street Repair (700), Street Cleaning (686), and Misc-Refuse Div (648), totaling 3,831. None maps by title to the 32 retrieved speed-control, sidewalk, or forestry Work Order searches. That title comparison does not prove that no back-end completion record exists.
