# Cityworks endpoint: new data and the gaps it actually narrows

## Summary

**Observed:** the endpoint is useful. All of its work-order searches have now been retrieved using only operational fields: **32 searches, 36,490 search memberships, and 29,018 distinct work orders**. Overlapping records agree on status, finish date, and closure date. The new material includes actual sidewalk-repair dates, completed tree work, and full open-work queues previously represented by capped samples. [Query service](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

**Observed:** sidewalk work supplies **1,627 actual finish dates**, including **633 finishes in 2025–26**. Of **186 recent hand-off requests**, **16** have a later repair at the same normalized address; all are after the request's promise date. Tree trimming adds **74 same-address completion candidates**. These are candidate request links, not verified foreign-key matches. Separately, the scoped active-request extract contains **19,291 requests**, with matches spanning **22 routing labels** in the public file—not verified distinct departments. [Sidewalk work](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78), [active requests](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/18)

**Inferred:** this can materially strengthen the proposal's completion evidence and queue visibility, but it does not supply completion ledgers for every department. Direct request-to-work-order keys remain missing; absence from a saved search is not evidence of unfinished work. Two completion-source gaps are substantially narrowed: sidewalks and tree trimming. Broader tree removal, water, towing, refuse, cave-ins, and signal/light completion gaps remain. The existing headline traceability percentage has not been overwritten.

A bounded scan of **80 public maps** found those references but no other QE references. Publication intent for other layers was not established by that scan; the later authorized operational extraction does not depend on claiming that every layer was deliberately published. **Observed:** the catalog has **87 entries**: **32 work-order, 42 inspection, 8 service-request, and 5 case-object searches**. Forty inspection entries repeat ten sets of four PCA priorities. The catalog is a set of saved searches, not a department inventory. [Catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

Every full extract checked numeric object-id ordering, non-overlapping pages, unique ids, and counts before and after retrieval. **All 42 authorized full extracts passed those checks.** Raw data and exact timestamped request URLs are under ignored `data/raw/cityworks-gap/authorized/`; a source-by-source row-count/date-range index is in [the retrieval manifest](cityworks-retrieval-manifest.csv). Public artifacts contain aggregate results, not work-order/request ids or property addresses. [Catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

### Sidewalk joins

**Observed:** the new sidewalk source fixes the earlier *source-availability* gap: the old sidewalk inventory's empty repair-date field is no longer the only option. There are **633 finish dates in 2025–26**, with no finish on a calendar date before work-order initiation and no future finish dates in this layer. At timestamp precision, one finish precedes initiation by about 50 minutes, and a different order closes about 10 seconds before its finish; neither affects the request candidates. Of **2,057 requests filed from 2025 through the local snapshot**, **49** have a later repair at the same normalized address, involving **41 distinct work orders**. All **49** are also within **25 metres**; the maximum separation is **4.535 metres**. None of their work finish stamps equals the corresponding 311 closure stamp. [Work orders](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78), [311 data](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Observed:** among **186 closed hand-off requests**, **16** match by address (**15 work orders**), all after the promise, with a median **179.5 days** between promise and recorded finish. The spatial sensitivity adds one candidate, giving **17**. Among **183** hand-offs whose promise was at least **30 days** past at the original analysis cutoff, the same **16** match. [Work orders](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78), [311 data](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Inferred limitations:** a repair at the same place after filing is strong candidate evidence, but it does not establish the city's actual request/order relationship. Multiple requests can refer to one repair. Address normalization strips unit information, and coordinate agreement may reflect shared geocoding. Different finish and closure timestamps reject literal duplication but do not alone prove independent data provenance. Only a small fraction of requests labeled done in 311 match this source, so unmatched hand-offs cannot be called undone. The dates alone do not establish whether the citizen-service promise is an appropriate operational target for the sidewalk program. [Analysis and aggregates](cityworks-sidewalk-aggregates.json)

## Tree work: stronger trimming evidence, removal still incomplete

**Observed:** the forestry searches yield **27,267 distinct work orders**, including **16,778 in open statuses** and **10,489 CLOSED**. The open queue is substantially larger than the old capped samples. However, **94** open-status orders already carry an actual finish date, and **1,750** forestry work orders have finish timestamps before initiation (**565** with finishes in the comparison period). **Inferred:** these sequences may reflect retrospective entry or inconsistent fields; they require separate status, chronology, and completion checks. The request-matching analysis conservatively excludes finishes before work-order initiation. [Forestry analysis](cityworks-forestry-workorders.md), [catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

**Observed:** of **140** recent requests converted to trimming work orders, **74** have a same-normalized-address completion candidate with compatible work type and work-order initiation near the request lifecycle. Of those matched requests, **6** have recorded finishes by the promise and **68** after it; the latter have a median **268.5 days** after the promise. Including a **30 projected-metre** spatial sensitivity produces **89** completion candidates. These are candidate relationships, not direct request/order links. [Forestry analysis](cityworks-forestry-workorders.md), [trim search](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/98)

**Inferred:** the newly retrieved closed searches materially improve tree-trimming evidence. They do not provide a general closed all-city removal ledger. The rolling trim search omits early completions from the comparison period; delay statistics among surviving matches must not be interpreted as a representative tree-service performance rate. The previous claim that most removal orders had closed was based on absence from an incomplete open-order sample; even the complete current open search cannot establish closure or removal for absent records. The dated completion candidates and recheck of the original removal cohort are documented in [the forestry report](cityworks-forestry-workorders.md).

## Queue visibility across services

**Observed:** the active-request extraction covers **61 selected operational codes**, excluding the register's lead, homeless-outreach, and logging-only nuisance categories. It returned **19,291 unique requests**, of which **18,993** match the existing 311 file. Those matches have **22 non-null division labels**. Routing labels include subdivisions and historical values; they must not be relabeled as verified departments. [Active SRs](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/18), [full queue report](cityworks-active-queues.md)

**Observed:** newly accessible routing/dispatch fields are populated: `DateSubmitTo` on **19,261** rows, `DateDispatchTo` on **12,883**, and `DateDispatchOpen` on **7,995**. Their business meanings are not yet established well enough to reproduce first-dispatch or CityStat routing latency. **9,165** records still open in the local 311 file have `Completed=1`; that flag must not be interpreted as field work completed. These service-request schemas expose no actual-work finish date. [Active SRs](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/18)

## Negative and limiting results preserved

- **Observed:** the public ARPA layers returned **663 scheduled** and **217 completed-category** records; all **880** join directly to 311, but filings are from **2014–2023**, so none adds to the current filing cohort. **649** scheduled-category rows are CLOSED; **636** say Work Completed in 311. This may reflect stale categorization or another unpublished queue rule, not unperformed repairs. The returned close dates duplicate 311 rather than independently dating repair. [Scheduled](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/19), [completed category](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/20)
- **Observed:** speed-control searches returned **13** funded/in-process orders and no actual finish dates; the new, dispatched, and estimated searches were empty. These do not close the traffic-signal/sign repair gap. [Funded speed control](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/14)
- **Observed:** the sidewalk inspection search returned **1,011 inspection completions**. That is evidence of inspection, not sidewalk replacement. [Inspections](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/79)
- **Observed:** sidewalk and utility permit metadata advertise issue/creation/modification dates but no actual-work completion field; no permit rows were retrieved. These schemas do not supply the missing water or street-repair completion ledger. [Sidewalk permits](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/80), [utility permits](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/88)
- **Observed:** public `/cw/` schemas advertise `RequestIds`, but **five-row probes of three searches** did not return that field. The first strict probe rejected unexpected operational columns before persistence; its request time was not logged and the failure event was reconstructed from captured execution output. Subsequent requests explicitly included those already-published columns. A schema field is not proof that a saved search will return it. [IRA map configuration](https://geoportal.stlouis-mo.gov/portal/sharing/rest/content/items/f2332491fba944878d838f8141407b97/data?f=json)
- **Inferred:** absence from a saved search, CLOSED status, an inspection finish date, and a work-order actual finish date answer different questions. None should substitute for the others.

## Reproducibility and integration

Run the existing scripts in `research/cityworks-gap-scripts/`; the analysis scripts use saved rows and an in-memory database attached read-only to `build/stl.duckdb`. Full endpoint refreshes are separate, explicit extraction calls. Exact query URLs, retrieval times, response hashes, field lists, and source counts are saved beside raw data. Independent review replayed all full-extract pages and reproduced the sidewalk joins. It found a log-classification bug: responses containing both features and a count were labeled count-only. Classification is now corrected from saved payloads, with original logs retained; source payloads and retrieval timestamps are unchanged. [Manifest](cityworks-retrieval-manifest.csv)

The established site, original reports, primary database, and original traceability headline were left unchanged. New results live under `research/` until source coverage and candidate-link uncertainty are reflected in the project's claim definitions. The complete service-by-service assessment is [the gap matrix](cityworks-department-gap-matrix.md). Retrieval and analysis checks are recorded in [the independent review](cityworks-independent-review.md).
