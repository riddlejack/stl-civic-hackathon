# Independent review of authorized Cityworks extracts

## Summary

**Observed.** A local-only replay of the saved artifacts found that all **42 authorized extracts**—32 Work Order, four Service Request and six Inspection layers—have equal before/after/source-row counts, unique and increasing page keys, and record fields exactly limited to the approved model projections. The corrected request logs now distinguish feature responses from count-only responses while preserving the original logs and payload hashes. These checks establish faithful retrieval of the saved-search results, not snapshot isolation or complete Cityworks enterprise coverage. [QE service](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

**Observed.** The sidewalk analysis reproduces the reported counts, but it remains candidate linkage. Among 2,057 recent sidewalk requests, 49 have a later same-normalized-address work-order finish; the 49 requests map to 41 work orders, and six work orders are reused across multiple requests. Sixteen of 186 hand-off/converted closures have a same-address candidate. No request-link field was returned, so the extracts cannot establish a direct request-to-work-order relationship. [Sidewalk work orders](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78) [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Observed.** The active-request extracts reproduce the published queue and workflow-field counts but do not supply an actual field-work finish. The final forestry analysis also reproduces from the saved rows: 74 of 572 converted requests have a chronology-valid finish candidate at the same normalized address, but the available searches are incomplete by operation and time. These are candidate links and cannot support request-level attribution or service-wide performance claims. [Active SRs](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/18) [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip) [Forestry QE98](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/98)

## Extraction contract and replay

**Observed.** Every saved feature row has exactly the requested allowlist keys; no staff, person, contact, comment, detail or `Description` field is present in the record payloads. Metadata field names were not treated as record values. Page records are strictly increasing and unique within each layer, concatenated row files preserve that order, count-before and count-after files agree with the summaries, and no returned geometry was persisted. [QE service](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

**Observed.** The logging correction reclassifies 88 extraction pages and ten bounded PCA probes as feature queries because their saved responses contain `features`; the earlier `count_only` label came from those same responses also containing a `count` member. All 98 corrected entries retain their original retrieval timestamps and match the saved payload hashes, and every changed log has an untouched `.original.jsonl` copy. [QE service](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

**Inferred limitation.** Equal counts around keyset pagination and a monotonic unique result strongly support a complete replay of each saved search. They cannot prove that a live source did not exchange one record for another between the two count calls, and the saved-search definitions remain opaque. Counts should therefore be described as complete retrievals of the observed saved-search memberships, not a complete department or enterprise inventory. [QE service](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

## Sidewalk candidate analysis

**Observed.** The underlying layer contains 1,738 unique work orders, 1,627 with `ActualFinishDate`, including 633 finishes from 1 January 2025 through 13 October 2026 Central time. All 49 exact-address candidates are also within 25 metres of the request coordinates; the maximum reported separation is 4.535 metres. That agreement is a consistency check, not an independent link, because address and coordinates may come from related geocoding inputs. [Sidewalk work orders](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78)

**Observed.** The date conversion correctly treats ArcGIS milliseconds as UTC and converts them to `America/Chicago`; matched service-request initiation times agree with the public 311 timestamps to the second. One sidewalk finish precedes its initiation by about 50 minutes on the same date, and a different record closes about ten seconds before its finish. The date-grain candidate rule excludes neither solely for same-day ordering, but neither anomalous work order participates in a reported sidewalk candidate. [Sidewalk work orders](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78) [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Inferred limitation.** Exact normalization removes unit suffixes and standardizes only a limited set of street forms, creating possible false positives at multi-unit locations and false negatives for other variants. The other-service comparison is contextual rather than a calibrated placebo because its geography, service mix, lifecycle and address density differ from sidewalk requests. A nonmatch cannot establish that no repair occurred because the saved search may omit work orders and 111 returned CLOSED rows lack an actual finish date. [Sidewalk work orders](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/78)

## Active service-request boundary

**Observed.** The active-request view contains 19,291 unique scoped requests, of which 18,993 join exactly to the public 311 file. Yet 9,165 exact matches that remain open in public 311 carry `Completed=1`, showing that this flag is not proof of field-work completion. One projected-completion value uses the year 1900 and must be treated as a sentinel. [Active SRs](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/18) [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Open.** City documentation for the workflow timestamps and `Completed` flag was not supplied. The defensible use is queue membership and named workflow milestones; latency, first-dispatch and physical-completion claims remain unsupported. [Active SRs](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/18)

## Forestry work orders and candidate links

**Observed.** The 27 Forestry saved searches contain 34,739 memberships and 27,267 unique work orders; 4,573 work orders occur in more than one search, with zero conflicts across the compared status, date, template, address and coordinate fields. This establishes a consistent saved-search union, not a department inventory. Across the union, 1,750 finish timestamps precede work-order initiation and one finish carries invalid year 4152, so the analysis correctly requires a chronology-valid finish rather than treating every populated finish field as usable. [QE catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json) [Forestry QE98](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/98)

**Observed.** Among 572 converted tree-work-order requests, 88 have a compatible same-normalized-address candidate and 74 have a finish on or after both request filing and work-order initiation. Six exact-address candidates finish by the request promise and 68 finish later, with a 268.5-day median delay. The broader address-or-30-EPSG:3857-projected-metre sensitivity has 89 valid finishes, all from the 140 trimming requests, and yields six by promise and 83 later. The coordinate threshold is a projected-coordinate sensitivity rather than a ground-distance claim. [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip) [Forestry QE98](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/98) [Forestry QE99](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/99)

**Observed.** Full-timestamp and `America/Chicago` calendar-date promise comparisons give the same six-by-promise, 68-late exact result and the same six-by-promise, 83-late sensitivity result; zero candidate finishes occur later than a promise timestamp on that same calendar date. The Forestry cohort of 572 and the matrix cohort of 565 are different populations: the former includes converted city- and private-tree requests, while the latter uses city-tree converted and hand-off closures. Their denominators should not be compared as if one were missing seven records. [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip)

**Observed.** Rechecking the prior 294-request 2025 removal cohort against the extracted current-open union finds eight exact-address candidates and ten address-or-coordinate candidates. That result supports only limited current-open membership: the extracts contain no general closed all-city removal search, so they cannot establish that the unmatched requests were completed. The same source constraint affects timing: QE98's calendar-valid finish window begins 14 October 2025, selecting the recoverable finish population. A contextual placebo finds 87 candidates among 29,797 unrelated service requests, but its different service mix, geography and lifecycle make it a co-location reference rather than a causal false-positive rate. [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip) [Forestry QE63](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/63) [Forestry QE98](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/98) [Forestry QE108](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/108)

**Observed.** The blank-cancellation sensitivity is internally disjoint: among 5,201 requests, 510 have a candidate already-open removal order, comprising 369 exact-address matches and another 141 projected-coordinate-only matches. This can support duplicate or already-queued work as one possible pathway, but supplies no completion evidence. [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip) [Forestry QE63](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer/63)

**Inferred limitation.** No returned work-order field provides a direct request key. Normalized-address agreement can merge multi-unit locations, spatial agreement is not independent when both systems inherit related geocoding, and operation labels are inferred from a partial template dictionary and narrow saved searches. Exact attribution requires a documented crosswalk or foreign key unavailable in these extracts. [311 source](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/upload/data-files/csb.zip) [QE catalog](https://service.stlouis-mo.gov/cityworks/gis/1/1/rest/services/qe/FeatureServer?f=json)

## Review boundary

This review read only local saved data and code. It made no network request, did not inspect or publish exact addresses or record identifiers, and did not test current server state after extraction. It independently recomputed the consequential extraction, sidewalk, active-request and forestry figures within the saved artifacts; it did not establish the undocumented meanings of Cityworks workflow fields or the completeness of opaque saved-search definitions.
