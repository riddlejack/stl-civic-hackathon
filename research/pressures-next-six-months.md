# G. Pressures on the City of St. Louis, 13 October 2026 – 30 April 2027

## Summary

**Found.** About 90 dated items fall between 13 Oct 2026 and 30 Apr 2027. Those
with a stated consequence:

- **ARPA, 31 Dec.** $35.6M of $498.1M was unprocessed on 6 Oct; invoices are due
  1 Nov; the rest is "subject to clawback by the U.S. Treasury". A separate $15M
  of demolition money has the same forfeiture date.
- **HUD, 15 Nov.** The 2027 plan for about $24.7M must reach HUD; its bill was
  still in committee on 13 Oct.
- **Police.** The mandatory share of general revenue rises from 23% to 24% on
  1 Jan; the state-appointed board certifies the FY2028 police budget by 28 Feb
  and the city must appropriate it. No ruling after June 2026 was found.
- **Winter.** No 2026-27 snow or shelter plan was posted on 13 Oct; shelter money
  is $2.2M against $3.36M; tornado rental aid for about 391 people ends in
  November; water shut-offs have resumed.
- **Ballots.** 3 Nov 2026; 2 Mar and 6 Apr 2027. Last day to introduce a bill
  is 8 Jan; ballot notice is due 26 Jan.
- **Capacity.** 20.7% of 6,420 budgeted positions were vacant; 18,415 service
  requests from 2025-26 were open; 52 of 2,674 tornado home repairs were done.
- **Cost.** About $43M a year in claims paid; $55.75M in police verdicts unpaid
  (reported).
- **Control.** Police, elections, courts, schools, sewers, transit and utilities
  sit outside the mayor's control; the mayor holds one of three votes on the
  board that approves every appropriation.

**Not established.** Any lawsuit status after June 2026; which projects hold the
unspent ARPA; whether the FY2025 Single Audit was filed; whether $32.4M of
road and bridge money was forfeited on 30 Sep; the cost of the January 2025
snow failure; dates for any of the mayor's 25 initiatives; the vendors behind
311, CityStat and the permit portal. Section 10 has the full list.

## 2. Hard deadlines

Detail: `G-money-deadlines.md`, `G-elections-legislation.md`, `G-legal-audit-cost.md`.

**Federal and state money**

- ARPA. $498,076,054 allocated; $462,482,252 processed by 6 Oct 2026 (observed [ARPA]). The city's project table, with data to 31 Jul 2026, showed $95.0M unspent across 197 projects, $24.2M of it in one SLDC project, North City Commercial Corridors (observed rows, sums inferred; 418 project rows and 35,495 expenditure rows retrieved 2026-10-13 23:29 UTC from the city ARPA pages). The split of the later $35.6M is not published.
- HUD formula grants. 15 Nov plan submission; timeliness test about 1-2 Nov; CAPER 31 Mar. The FY2024 Single Audit found the city missed the ESG 180-day obligation rule by 104-141 days, a repeat finding (observed [FAC]).
- Continuum of Care. The city "remains a recipient of CoC funds for 18 projects"; the collaborative-applicant role moved to a nonprofit; the FY2027 budget assumes CoC and ESG of $14.2M, "a decrease of $2.8 million" (observed). Grant expiry dates were not found.
- Tornado. No CDBG-DR exists for the 2025 tornado (reported); the mayor is seeking "$1 billion to $1.5 billion". The state reappropriated $180,660,571 of its $186M for the year ending 30 Jun 2027, which implies little was spent in the first year (bill text observed ✓; implication inferred). 2022-flood CDBG-DR: $303,306 of $25,834,000 drawn by 30 Jun 2026 (observed in HUD's quarterly report).
- Roads and bridges. See the 30 Sep 2026 row above. Bids for two of the bridges are due 20 Oct and 10 Nov.
- Financial reporting. FY2026 audit to the State Auditor 31 Dec; Single Audit 31 Mar. The city is not a "low-risk auditee" (observed [FAC]). Airport revenue bonds of up to $500M (Board Bill 51) passed 9 Oct 2026; sale date not found.

**Courts**

- No consent decree, ADA order or environmental order against the city was found.
- Police funding. City and Budget Director v. State and Police Board, filed 9 Apr 2026: no reported ruling. The city won the FY2026 round on 2 Jun 2026 (no extra $68M owed); Cole County rulings of 12 and 15 Jun 2026 upheld the state-control law and plaintiffs said they would appeal (all reported). Missouri court websites returned 403 to automated requests, so nothing after June is verified.
- Jail. Workhouse class settlement of $4M: claims closed 29 Jun 2026; final approval not confirmed (reported).
- Sheriff. The elected sheriff was removed; a court-appointed interim is still listed on the city page (observed 13 Oct); the federal criminal case has no trial date (reported).

**Audits**

- State Auditor: audits of SLMPD (from 7 Jan 2026) and the Building Division (announced 26 Mar 2026) are in progress with no release date (observed).
- Comptroller Internal Audit, 15 reports read: CEMA's separation-of-duties finding is rated HIGH and unresolved since 2024, with sirens "Not Tested" (28 Apr 2026); Towing has six findings and 13 earlier ones "Not resolved" (26 Mar 2025); three correction dates have passed (calendar above). Observed [AUDITDB].
- The outside tornado review said the 2003 emergency plan must be updated "to be in compliance with federal and state law" (observed); CEMA's commissioner said in May 2026 that it waits on new staff (reported). No date exists.

**Legislation, charter, elections**

- Pending bills with dates in them: BB 86 (HUD plan); BB 91 (Code Blue programme, introduced 9 Oct, would take effect 1 Apr 2027); BB 78 (unallocated ward street money reappropriated on 1 Jan 2027); BB 59 (transit tax, held in committee 8 Oct). Observed in bill text.
