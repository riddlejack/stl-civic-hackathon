# Names: DoneStat and five alternatives

Drafted by N7, 14 Oct 2026, about 1:00 a.m. Central. Jack decides. Rules: plain words, no city marks (no seal, no "St. Louis", no "STL", no arch). The clash check was eight keyless web searches run at 12:55 a.m. That is not a trademark search; one is worth running before anything is registered or hosted.

## Recommendation: keep DoneStat

It says what the thing measures (done, not closed) and puts it in the "Stat" family the city already runs. That family is Baltimore's CitiStat and its copies, and Martin O'Malley visited St. Louis CityStat in July 2026. The CityStat team will know at once where it fits. The searches found no product, project or tool called DoneStat. Two weaker points:

- It leans on CityStat's name. That is fine for something the CityStat team would run. It would be wrong if the product were ever presented as the city's own; the footer already says it is not.
- "Done" alone is a crowded and partly unlucky brand: Done Global is an ADHD telehealth company whose founders were convicted of fraud in 2025. "DoneStat" as one word avoids confusion with it.

Name the resident-facing piece "the receipt" whatever the product is called. It is the plainest word for what a resident gets.

## The six names

| Name | What it says | Clash found (searched 14 Oct 2026, 12:55 a.m.) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DoneStat** (working) | A CityStat-style measure of done work | None by that name. Nearest: the Linux tool `dstat`; Done Global (telehealth); the PerformanceStat family (CitiStat, CapStat, ATL Stat, StatGPT), which is the right neighbourhood | **Recommended** |
| **Promise Kept** | Graded against the city's own promised date | None by that name in civic tech. But "Promise Tracker" tools (Code for Africa / PesaCheck; Mzalendo Trust, Kenya) grade politicians' campaign pledges, and Promise is a US govtech payments firm | Strong idea, wrong overtone: it sounds like grading the mayor's campaign promises, which is the "audit" objection in a name |
| **Follow-Through** | Did the city finish what it started | None found for a 311 tool | Plain and accurate, but too generic to search for or own |
| **DoneCheck** | Check whether a job was done | None exact; near-misses are to-do apps (DoCheck, Indonesia; "Done: Checklist" on iOS) | Sounds like a to-do app |
| **City Receipt** | A receipt for each request | None found as a 311 product | Good for the resident feature, too narrow for the weekly measure and the lists |
| **Was It Done?** | The question the product answers | None found. It echoes San Diego's 311 app "Get It Done", which a city audit found sometimes gave residents confusing or inaccurate information about their requests | Memorable, but it reads as a dig at Get It Done and as a question rather than a product |
