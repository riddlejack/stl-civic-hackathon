## Title

Follow-Through STL: when St. Louis 311 says "closed", is the work done? (Civic AI Hackathon 2026)

## Description

St. Louis 311 closes a request when it hands the job to a crew, and after that nothing tracks whether the work gets done. I audited 1.45 million 311 requests, 2.25 million Forestry billing records, the city's Cityworks work orders and 1,440 of its status pages, joined request by request.

The fix needs no new 311 system:
- tie each request to its crew's job;
- keep the request open until the job is done;
- flag every request past its date with no finished job;
- put the most dangerous work first.

It runs as plain SQL and saved searches inside the systems the city already uses (Cityworks, CityStat and the 311 status page). The website in the video is a demo of that output, running on real city data.

Code, analysis, sources and the hand-over package: https://github.com/riddlejack/stl-civic-hackathon
