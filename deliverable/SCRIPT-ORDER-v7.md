## 1. Your question: does closing early make the city lose track?

**Yes, for closed requests, and it is provable where crews keep records.** But the big overdue numbers are a second, different failure.

| | Closed at the hand-off | Left open |
|---|---|---|
| What the resident and CityStat see | "Closed", about 2 days after filing | "Open", often for months |
| What's actually known about the work | Where crews keep records (mowing, board-ups): 18% done by the promised date; about half late (**observed**, `research/chapters/R1-trace.md`). The North Taylor lot: closed twice, no cut on record since Oct 2024. 28% of 2026 "on schedule to be cut" answers went to properties with no cut on record all year (**observed**, `research/north-taylor-mechanism.md`) | 14,902 past the date: 3% show the work done, 8% have a crew job pending, **84% have no record either way** (**observed**, `research/open-queue-reality-check.md`) |
| The failure | **Closed doesn't mean done.** The job can drop, and nothing notices. | **Open doesn't mean tracked.** The city can't tell which are done, waiting or lost. |
| The cause | The request isn't tied to the crew's job | Same |

**What that means for the numbers you wanted tied in** (the 2,778 life-safety requests and the 1,630 trees):
- **The 2,778 are open, not closed.** The honest tie is that **for 2,562 of them (92%), no record says whether a crew has been out** (**observed**, `research/impact-quantification.md` §4b). That links them to the tracking problem without saying they were closed early.
- **The 1,630 tree removals are the counter-example.** Forestry does link them, and they're still late (a median of 618 days past Forestry's own finish date). So they can't be shown as lost.
  - What they show is that linking makes the lateness *countable*; it doesn't make crews faster.
  - The draft therefore uses 1,630 only as the tree baseline in the test (beat 13).
- **The contrast with cave-ins makes the point.** Nothing is attached on 0 of 30 cave-in pages read (**observed**, E1), so nobody can say which of the 1,233 overdue cave-ins are fixed.

### Act 3: What a fix needs

*Title:* **One cause, four requirements: tie the job · keep it open · flag the misses · danger first**

*Optional closing sentence* (your Round 2 "why not the alternatives") [15]: "A hotspot map or a chatbot would sit on top of the same broken loop."

*Title:* **Already done in St. Louis for tree removals, and the rule in Chicago, Houston and Toronto**
